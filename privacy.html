Privacy

Who we are: Gil Zumbrunnen (Zum Productions), based in Switzerland. Contact: privacy@zumproductions.com.

Visiting this website: Our hosting provider, Infomaniak Network SA in Switzerland, automatically records technical data when you visit: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. This data is used only to run and secure the site. Infomaniak keeps these logs for a short period that it sets (at least 7 days), and copies in its hosting backups are deleted after 7 days. We use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and we load nothing from third parties.

Emailing us: If you write to us at hello@zumproductions.com or privacy@zumproductions.com, we use your address and message only to reply. Our email is hosted by Microsoft (Microsoft 365), and the data is stored within the EU/EFTA under Microsoft's EU Data Boundary. We delete emails when they're no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Our games: see the separate Games privacy policy.

Where data is processed: Website data is processed in Switzerland. Email data is processed in Switzerland or the EU/EEA, which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate data protection.

Your rights: you can ask for access to your data, correction or deletion, and object to processing. You can also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
